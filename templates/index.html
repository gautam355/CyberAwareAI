The 4-Stage Social Engineering Attack Chain (Visual Infographic)

How an attacker moves from target reconnaissance to complete credential compromise.

DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH
01 Reconnaissance

The Craft & Context

Attacker scouts your department on LinkedIn. Crafts an email mimicking tools you use daily (e.g. Microsoft 365, Slack, or HR Payroll).

Vector: Phishing Email
02 Cognitive Hijack

Emotional Urgency Hook

Panic is introduced: *"Account expires in 2 hours"* or *"Salary deposit held"*. This bypasses logical thinking and induces rushed action.

Tactic: Artificial Urgency
03 Execution

Deceptive Redirection

User clicks the fake button. The link routes to a cloned replica login page engineered to look 100% identical to your corporate portal.

Tactic: Credential Harvesting
04 Compromise

Session Hijacking

Attacker intercepts username, password, and session token. Lateral movement commences inside corporate infrastructure.

Result: Account Breach

AUTHENTIC CORPORATE EMAIL Safe Verification
Sender: it-support@yourcompany.com (Official Domain)
Salutation: "Dear Gautam," (Personalized name from HR database)
Link URL: https://login.microsoftonline.com/auth (Standard Official SSL)
Tone: Clear grace period (14 days notification with IT contact extension)
ADVERSARY PHISHING CLONE High Hazard
Sender: support@micros0ft-portal-auth.com (Typosquatted domain)
Salutation: "Dear Employee / Team Member" (Generic automated greeting)
Link URL: http://login-microsoft365-verify.com/auth (Disposable unverified host)
Tone: Immediate panic ("Immediate suspension in 2 hours!")
